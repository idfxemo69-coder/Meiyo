Features

Made for the everyday
running of a server.

No giant promises. Just a set of practical tools for member arrivals, invite activity, and server housekeeping.

01

Welcome messages

Give new members a clear, friendly introduction when they arrive.

02

Invite tracking

Attribute joins to invite links where Discord makes that information available.

03

Invite leaderboard

See invite totals and recognise the people helping your server grow.

04

Automatic roles

Assign a configured role to members when they join.

05

Join and leave logs

Keep useful member arrival and departure events visible to your team.

06

Server settings

Configure supported channels, roles, and message options for your server.

A note about Meiyo

Useful, quiet,
out of the way.

Meiyo is built around a simple idea: community tools should help the people running a server without becoming the centre of attention.

Use the features that make sense for your space, configure them to fit, and let the bot take care of the repeatable bits.
