The ground rules for a friendly, fair, and welcoming gaming community.

1. Welcome to Meiyo

Meiyo is a friendly gaming community built around respectful conversation, finding people to play with, and enjoying games together. By joining or participating in our Discord server, you agree to these community terms.

2. Be respectful

  • Treat members with respect. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, and targeted abuse are not allowed.
  • Do not spam, flood channels, raid the server, or disrupt other members' conversations.
  • Do not share another person's private information, account credentials, or personal content without permission.
  • Keep content in the appropriate channels and follow moderator instructions.

3. Safe and lawful use

  • Do not use the community to promote scams, phishing, malware, cheating services that compromise others, or other harmful activity.
  • Do not post illegal content or content that violates Discord's rules.
  • Respect intellectual property and do not impersonate other members, staff, or official services.
  • Use age-appropriate channels and follow applicable age requirements.

4. Moderation

Moderators may remove content, issue warnings, restrict access, mute, kick, or ban members when reasonably necessary to protect the community. Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate action. Moderation decisions are made to maintain a safe and welcoming space.

5. Meiyo bot

Meiyo may provide welcome messages, invite tracking, invite leaderboards, account-age notices, and optional role assignment. These features may be changed, paused, or discontinued. Invite counts are best-effort estimates and may not always be perfectly accurate.

6. Your responsibility

You are responsible for your account activity and for ensuring that the content you share is appropriate and that you have permission to share it. Do not attempt to exploit, disrupt, or reverse-engineer the bot or its hosting environment.

7. Discord's rules also apply

Our server operates on Discord. You must also comply with the Discord Terms of Service and Discord Community Guidelines. Those documents govern your use of Discord's platform; these terms describe the additional rules for participating in the Meiyo community.

8. Changes

These terms may be updated as the community develops. Continued participation after an updated version is posted means you accept the revised terms. Material changes should be announced in the server.

9. Contact

For questions, concerns, or moderation appeals, contact the Meiyo server administration team through the official Discord server.

These community terms are a practical starting point, not legal advice. Review them for your actual server rules and local legal requirements before publishing.
